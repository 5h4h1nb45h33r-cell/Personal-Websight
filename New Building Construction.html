3.5 Cent 1350 Sq Feet Double Story Residential Building

3.5 Cent 1350 Sq Feet Double Story Residential Building

This expertly crafted 1350 sq. ft. double-story residential building, located at Neerkode (Kallupalam), Aluva, is a fine example of modern space optimization built thoughtfully on a compact 3.5-cent plot. Designed for comfortable family living, the ground floor features a well-defined layout comprising a car porch, welcoming sit-out, dedicated visiting room, dining space, modern kitchen, and 1 bedroom with an attached bathroom. The first floor houses 2 spacious bedrooms—each with attached bathrooms—and seamlessly connects to a scenic balcony and a thoughtfully designed open terrace, ideal for outdoor relaxation. Combining smart spatial planning with modern aesthetics, this project highlights our commitment to delivering high-quality, functional homes tailored to urban land dimensions.

Key upgrades included complete flooring overhaul, wall strengthening, modern kitchen remodeling, and premium exterior elevation painting and lighting.

  • ✔ Electrical & Plumbing Piping

  • ✔ Sanitary & Fitting Installation
